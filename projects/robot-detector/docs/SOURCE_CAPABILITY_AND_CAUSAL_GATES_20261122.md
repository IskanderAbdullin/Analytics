## Source and causality evidence

1. **Official LOBSTER output semantics**: The vendor says message row k causes the transition from orderbook row k−1 to book row k; therefore row k is **post-event**, not available as a pre-event feature for the corresponding message. A raw post-event snapshot at the same timestamp as a public trade cannot enter `lambda(t-)`. Source: https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/DataStructure.php (accessed 2026-11-22).
2. **Official precision boundary**: Vendor documentation states seconds after midnight, decimal precision at least milliseconds and possibly nanoseconds depending on period; this does NOT attest that rounding to exact integer milliseconds retains every ordering relation. Per-file timestamp resolution, tied rows and loss from normalization must be inspected before treating its events as independent unique millisecond clusters. Same source as above.
3. **Official public sample**: Paired message+orderbook 10-level AMZN demo is offered by LOBSTER; independent use/redistribution/license interpretation must follow source terms rather than assume all downloadable material is commercially reusable. https://php.lobsterdata.com/info/DataSamples.php and existing [sample QA](LOBSTER_AMZN_PUBLIC_SAMPLE_FORMAT_AUDIT_20261121.md).
4. **ABIDES-NG code license**: BSD-3-Clause stated by upstream README (https://github.com/GabrieleDiCorato/abides-ng). A pinned simulator produces source *synthetic* observations, not independent evidence of real market agent identities or actual feed completeness.
5. Provider-specific permissions for Binance/MOEX/Coinbase, recorded at [REAL_SOURCE_LICENSE_GATES.md](REAL_SOURCE_LICENSE_GATES.md), remain active restrictions. No new acquisition or legal certification was performed.

## Negative consequences for current M0/M1/M1-v2/M2

- M0 can be technically scored on public timestamp clusters, but **origin-independent timing and coverage remain unverified** on the public real archives. This does not establish calibrated rejection or FDR.
- Original stable-window M1 requires an actual previous complete BBO and <1s source-age; it abstains on constructed longer-book windows. New M1-v2 requires a 1-second book update lag and externally asserted 10-second continuity/age. **Its source-completeness flag is a caller assertion, not source proof**; even a green 149-test CI cannot independently establish completeness.
- M2's full event-history compensator needs the **whole** public event process in the observation interval; missing trades or timestamp coarsening invalidates its theoretical residual interpretation. It cannot identify owners.
- The exact three-tape non-target held-out LL [negative](CROSSMODEL_HELDOUT_LL_NEGATIVE_20261122.md) showed M2 collapsed to M0 and M1-v2 scored worse. This does not justify choosing a favorable model/threshold or creating extra simulated seed runs.
- Frozen 160 ABIDES source labels remain useful **retrospective method failures**, not a new independent holdout. Reproducing M0/M1-v2/M2 requires a *prospectively frozen*, independently sourced new public tape with legal rights and source-quality evidence, not rebranding the old source summary.

## Decision / next scientific gate

**Do not allocate expensive new independent source seeds yet.** The proposed 100-seed evaluation is insufficient for strict joint familywise criteria under plausible nonzero false alarms ([power audit](INDEPENDENT_POWER_FEASIBILITY_AUDIT_20261122.md)). Before a new original ABIDES study: pin source revision and dependency hashes, define matched public-only event/book projection, prove or conservatively flag coverage/clock quality, choose training/calibration/evaluation original-world split and predictive score/abstention rules, assess sample-size feasibility and resource budget, freeze all choices **before** source generation. For real-market claims, independent owner labels and source license would still be required; absent those, issue #72 remains open, and an honest identifiability-limit result is scientifically valid.

No raw LOBSTER/Binance/COIN/MOEX source, broker credentials, agent IDs, original event rows or real orders were exported or fetched in this stage.
